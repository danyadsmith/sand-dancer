### Lessons Learned

* There are three main steps or stages required to finish an Inform project:
  * **Polishing** adds finishing touches, confirms everything has been implemented from the Concept Document, and completes missing pieces
  * **Testing** involves play testing the game looking for game and story issues
  * **Releasing** makes the game available to be discovered and played
